About

T AGENT

Tulga's personal agent. The tools it builds and runs for him live here: no ads, no subscriptions, the data stays his.

Fitness is a gym and body-weight tracker: plan your week, run guided workouts, log every set and your weight. It installs on your phone and signs you in with a passkey. Built on openGym (AGPL-3.0).

Nomadex is live horse race tracking: every horse on the course as the race runs, with positions, speed and results as they happen.

Move with the arrow keys, open with Enter, go back with Escape. Or just tap.
