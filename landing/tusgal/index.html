A real case

The wheel loader tender in Dornogovi

In January 2023 prosecutors sent this case to court. It shows the most common pattern in Mongolian procurement: the people who wanted a certain company to win also controlled the committee that chose it.

Buyer
Chandman-Ilch, a locally owned state company
Purchase
Wheel loaders for the Urgun branch
Loss to the state
₮102 million
Charge
Abuse of power, Criminal Code 22.1.2
The buyer decides it needs loadersThe director, supply manager and chief engineer agree on a company they want to win.
The buyer's own accountant chairs the committeeThe person judging the bids reports to the people who picked the favourite.
The favourite winsProsecutors say the group misused their positions to steer the award.
₮102 million is lostDiscovered only afterwards, by investigators.
January 2023: case goes to courtFour officials charged with abuse of power. They are presumed innocent until the court rules.
Source: Prosecutor's Office weekly report, via UBN, 16 Jan 2023. The report does not name the winning company or the bid prices.

How each part of Тусгал would have stopped it

casinoRandom committeestep 1

What happenedThe chair was the buyer's own chief accountant. The director's team judged its own tender.
With ТусгалMembers are drawn from a pool across agencies and aimags. The buyer's staff cannot sit, and nobody knows the names until the draw.
publicInternational practiceKorea's national e-procurement system (KONEPS) draws evaluators at random from a pool of thousands of registered experts and tells them only shortly before the meeting. Courts in many countries, including Mongolia, already assign judges to cases by electronic lottery for the same reason.

mailBlind scoringstep 2

What happenedThe committee knew which bid was the favourite and scored it up.
With ТусгалBids arrive as A, B and C. Scores are sealed before names open, so there is no favourite to push.
publicInternational practiceEU procurement uses a two-envelope rule: technical bids are scored before the price envelope is opened. Ukraine's ProZorro publishes every bid and score, so any citizen can re-check the result. Orchestras that moved to blind auditions behind a screen saw hiring change measurably.

flagRed flags before signingstep 3

What happenedNothing warned anyone. The loss was found later by investigators.
With ТусгалThe price is compared with what other state bodies paid for the same loader model, and the winner's owners are checked against the buyer's managers. Either raises an alert before signing.
publicInternational practiceThe European Commission's ARACHNE tool scores every EU-funded contract for risk using over 100 indicators. Brazil's audit court runs a robot called Alice that reads new tenders every night and flags suspicious ones to auditors before they are signed.

routeTraceable paymentstep 3

What happenedOnce paid, nobody could see whether money came back to the officials.
With ТусгалPaid in digital tögrög. Any onward payment to a company or relative linked to the director, engineer or accountant shows on the record.
publicInternational practiceUkraine's Spending.gov.ua publishes every payment from the state treasury, to every recipient, within days. Brazil's Transparency Portal has done the same for federal payments since 2004. China pays some civil servant salaries and subsidies in e-CNY, its central bank digital currency.

campaignWhistleblower rewardstep 4

What happenedPeople inside the company knew. Speaking would have risked their jobs for nothing.
With ТусгалA losing bidder or an employee reports anonymously. If the court recovers ₮102 million, they receive ₮10.2 million.
publicInternational practiceThe US False Claims Act pays whistleblowers 15 to 30% of recovered funds and has returned over 70 billion dollars since 1986. The US SEC pays 10 to 30% of sanctions above 1 million dollars. Korea's Anti-Corruption and Civil Rights Commission pays rewards of up to 3 billion won.

Run Тусгал on a sample tender

A ₮5 billion road repair with fictional companies. Press each step and watch the bar at the top fill.

1

Who will judge the tender?

12 trained evaluators from 6 agencies. Nobody knows which 5 will be chosen until the draw. Anyone with a family or business link to a bidder is removed first.

check_circle5 evaluators chosen at random. E07 was removed because a relative co-owns one of the bidding companies. The draw uses a public number, so anyone can repeat it and confirm it was fair.
2

Which bid is best?

The committee sees only price and quality, never the company name. Scores are sealed before the envelopes are opened.

check_circleThe winner was chosen on quality and price alone. Because the scores were sealed first, nobody could change them after seeing the names.
3

Where does the money go?

The winner is paid in a digital tögrög issued by the Bank of Mongolia. Every payment it makes onward stays on the record.
